Our Core Values

How we dive, teach and treat each other, in the water and out of it.

  1. Safety First

    Put the safety of your teammates and yourself as a top priority in and out of the water. Never cut corners at the expense of safety.

  2. Always Do the Right Thing

    Act with integrity, keep your word and be honest. Take responsibility for your actions and be worthy of trust. Speak up without fear of criticism if you have an idea or a concern no matter how large or small.

  3. Be A Team Player

    Strive to be positive, fun, and humble. Support your team members in all their endeavors.

  4. Grow Yourself and Others

    Take every opportunity to improve your diving skills and knowledge. Mentor others and pass on the expertise and knowledge you’ve earned to benefit the team.

  5. Celebrate Diversity

    We are many and varied, but we are all God’s children. Our differences make us stronger as a team.

  6. Respect Yourself and Others

    Believe that you are good and worthy of being treated well, and in doing so be respectful to others. No one has a right to disparage you. Be respectful of the sacrifices others have made to get us where we are today.
